KET	语法知识点-现在完成时
概念：①表示过去发生的或已完成的某一动作对现在造成的影响
②表示从过去某一时刻开始一直持续到现在的动作或状态，这个动作可能刚停止也可能仍然在进行
结构：①肯定句：主语+have/has+过去分词+其他
②否定句：主语+have/has+not+过去分词+其他
③疑问句：Have/Has+主语+过去分词+其他
时间提示词

ever（“曾经”，否定句疑问句）
already（“已经”，肯定句）
动词变化规则：
加ed：一般情况	work→worked	play→played
加d：以不发音的e结尾的词	hope→hoped	hate→hated
y变i加ed：以辅音字母加y结尾的词	study→studied
双写尾字母加ed：以重读闭音节结尾，未尾一个辅音字母
stop→stopped	drop→dropped

KET现在完成时时·练一练
一、单项选择题
1. I _ my homework since I got home.
A. finish	B. finished	C. have finished	D. was finishing
2. They _ the project since last week.
A. completed	B. have completed
C. had completed	D. were completing
3. She _ her keys in the office just now.
A. left	B. has left	C. is leaving	D. was leaving
4. We _ to the museum twice since the beginning of this year.
A. go	B. went	C. have gone	D. are going
5. The children _ their lunch by the time you arrived.
A. eat	B. ate	C. have eaten	D. are eating
6. She _ a new novel recently.
A. wrote	B. has written	C. is writing	D. was writing
